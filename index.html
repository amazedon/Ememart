📋 Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using Ememart, you agree to these terms. You must be 18+ years old to use this platform, especially for financial features and betting services.

2. User Roles & Conduct

Users must be honest in their listings. Vendors must deliver what they advertise. Fraudulent listings will result in permanent bans and penalties.

3. Escrow & Payments

Ememart holds escrow funds impartially. Fees are deducted at rates set by the admin. Disputes are resolved within 48 hours per our SLA.

4. VTU & Betting

VTU services are provided by third-party APIs. Betting fund top-ups carry a mandatory warning. Ememart is not liable for gambling losses. Set spending limits to protect yourself.

5. KYC Requirements

Financial features require KYC verification. A ₦100 fee applies to cover API costs. Manual review takes 24-48 hours. False documents will result in permanent bans.

6. Refunds & Disputes

See our Buyer Protection Guarantee for full refund policies. Admin decisions on disputes are final.

7. Changes

Ememart may update these terms at any time. Continued use means acceptance of new terms.

1. Data We Collect

We collect name, email, phone number, location (town, LGA, state), and BVN/NIN for KYC-verified users. We also collect transaction history and post content.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the platform, process payments, verify identity, and show relevant local content. We never sell your data to advertisers.

3. Data Sharing

We share necessary data with payment processors (Flutterwave/Paystack), KYC verification providers, and SMS/push notification services. We share nothing else without your consent.

4. Location Data

Your town/LGA/state is displayed on your public profile to enable hyperlocal search. You can update this anytime in Settings.

5. Security

All passwords are hashed. Transaction PINs are encrypted. Financial data is handled by PCI-compliant processors.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting support@ememart.ng. KYC data is retained for legal compliance for 7 years.

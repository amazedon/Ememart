Questions people ask

What is escrow?

Escrow means Ememart holds your payment while the order is being completed. The Partner gets paid only after you confirm, or automatically 48 hours after delivery if you do not respond.

What does it cost?

You can browse and post for free. Customers pay a small escrow fee on each order, and Partners pay a small fee when an order completes. The exact amounts are shown in the app before you pay.

Where can I use Ememart?

Ememart is built for Nigeria. Wallet funding and withdrawals work in Nigeria only.

Do I need to be verified to sell?

No. Anyone can sell without verification. Verifying with your NIN or CAC certificate earns a blue tick and raises your wallet limits.

What if my order does not arrive?

Message the Partner first. If that does not fix it, open a dispute in the app with photos. Your money stays in escrow until the dispute is decided.

How do I contact Ememart?

Open the app, go to Account, then Safety and Support, and start a live chat. You can also email help@ememart.com.

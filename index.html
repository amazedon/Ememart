Your money waits until your food arrives

When you order, your payment is held by Ememart. The vendor only gets paid after you confirm.

1

Pay into escrow

Choose your food and pay from your Ememart wallet. The vendor sees the order, not your money.

2

Vendor delivers

They mark the order as delivered. A rider or the vendor brings it to you.

3

You confirm

Tap confirm and the vendor is paid. If you do nothing, payment is released after 48 hours.

Something went wrong? Open a dispute with photos before the 48 hours end. Our support team reviews it and decides.

Three kinds of people on Ememart

Food vendors

Home cooks, restaurants and food sellers who post what they made today. Customers can also book big orders ahead of time for events.

Artisans

Tailors, barbers, mechanics, makeup artists and more. Customers find them by area and pay through Ememart.

Riders

Riders pick up deliveries near them and get paid when the customer confirms.

Put Ememart on your home screen

No app store and no big download. Add Ememart to your phone and it opens like any other app, even on a slow connection.

Or open it in the browser
  1. Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Install app.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

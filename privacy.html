1. Who we are

Ememart (“Ememart”, “we”, “us”) runs a local marketplace in Nigeria at www.ememart.com, available as a website, an installable web app and an Android app. It connects Customers (people who buy or order) with Partners (food Stores, Artisans and Riders).

For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (“NDPA”), Ememart is the data controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at help@ememart.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only in hashed form), profile photo and bio.
  • Location details: your town, local government area (LGA) and state, and, for Partners, business name and address or service area.
  • Posts and listings: photos, descriptions, prices, categories and other details Partners publish.
  • Orders and messages: what you order, delivery details, delivery codes, chat messages with other users, ratings and reviews.
  • Verification details: where a feature requires identity verification (for example wallet or Partner verification), we may collect documents or numbers such as a BVN or NIN, and any photos required for the check.
  • Support and dispute information: messages, evidence and photos you send when you contact us or open a dispute.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: device type, browser, app version, IP address, pages and features used, and error logs.
  • Approximate or precise location: only if you allow your device to share it, so we can show nearby Partners. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Push notification token: if you turn on notifications, so we can send order and message alerts.

Payment information

Card, bank transfer and USSD payments are handled by our payment provider, Squad (GTCO). We do not see or store your full card number or card PIN. We keep a record of your wallet balance, transactions and payment references.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. What is public

Some information is visible to other people by design:

  • Your name, profile photo, bio, town/LGA/state, verified badge, Partner type, average rating and member-since date appear on your profile if you are a Partner, and your name may appear on reviews you write.
  • Partners’ posts (photos, descriptions, prices) are visible to anyone using the app.
  • Your phone number is shared with a Partner or Rider only where needed to complete an order or where you choose to allow calls.

Your email, wallet balance, verification documents and chat messages are not public.

5. Who we share data with

We share only what is needed with:

  • Other users: order and delivery details are shared between the Customer, the Partner and the Rider handling that order.
  • Payment provider: Squad, to process payments and payouts.
  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions), Cloudinary (storage and delivery of post photos) and our hosting provider, who process data on our behalf.
  • Notification providers: browser and device push services (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple/Mozilla push services) to deliver alerts. Email and SMS providers, where used.
  • Verification providers: identity-check services, where a verification feature requires it.
  • Authorities and advisers: courts, regulators, law enforcement, or professional advisers when the law requires it or to protect rights, safety and property.
  • Business transfers: a successor, if Ememart is merged or sold, who must honour this policy.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens we take steps required by the NDPA, such as contractual safeguards, so your data keeps an adequate level of protection.

8. Security

We use passwords stored as hashes, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our database, and separate payment handling by a regulated provider. No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password, keep your delivery codes private, and never share them outside the app. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will act on it and notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data (subject to the retention periods above);
  • restrict or object to certain uses, including direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect past use);
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

To use these rights, email help@ememart.com from the email address on your account. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Delete your account and data

How to delete your account: email help@ememart.com with the subject “Delete my Ememart account”, from the email address registered on the account. Tell us the name on the account.

  • We will verify it is you, then delete your profile, posts, photos, messages and notification tokens within 30 days.
  • Withdraw any wallet balance first. We can help release it to your bank account.
  • Records we must keep by law (orders, payments, verification) are retained for the period in section 7 and are no longer used for anything else.
  • Reviews you wrote may stay in anonymised form so Partner ratings remain accurate.

11. Notifications and permissions

The app may ask for permission to use your location, camera or photo library (to upload post and profile photos and delivery proof), microphone (voice search, if you use it) and notifications. Each is optional and can be changed in your device or browser settings. Some features will not work without the related permission.

12. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage and a service worker to keep you signed in, remember preferences (such as theme and recent searches) and let the app load quickly. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

13. Children

Ememart is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a material change we will notify you in the app or by email and update the date at the top. Continued use after the change means you accept it.

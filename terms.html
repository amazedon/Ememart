1. About these Terms

These Terms and Conditions (“Terms”) govern your use of Ememart, a local marketplace in Nigeria that connects Customers with Partners. Partners are food Stores, Artisans and Riders. By creating an account or using Ememart you agree to these Terms and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use Ememart.

Ememart is a platform. Unless we say otherwise, the sale of food, the performance of a service and the delivery of an order are contracts between the Customer and the Partner (and the Rider where one is used), not with Ememart.

2. Eligibility and your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding contract under Nigerian law.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date. One person, one account.
  • Keep your password and transaction PIN secret. You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at once if you suspect misuse.
  • We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms or put others at risk.

3. Customers

  • Order and pay inside Ememart. Do not pay outside the app. Payments made outside Ememart are not protected.
  • Check the listing, price, delivery method and fees before you order. Give a correct delivery address and be reachable for delivery.
  • Never share your password or transaction PIN with anyone, including people who say they work for Ememart. Your 6-digit delivery code is the only code to share, and only with the Partner or Rider handing over your order, when you have received it. Sharing it releases your payment immediately.
  • Rate Partners honestly after a completed order.

4. Partners (Stores, Artisans and Riders)

  • Post only items and services you are allowed to sell and able to deliver. Descriptions, photos, prices and availability must be accurate.
  • Stores offer food only. Food must be safe, properly prepared, and comply with applicable food and hygiene rules. Artisans must have the skills they claim. Riders must hold any licence required for their vehicle and deliver orders safely.
  • Complete your profile before posting. Partners are independent businesses, not employees or agents of Ememart.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes, permits and licences.
  • Fulfil accepted orders on time. Repeated cancellations, late delivery or poor ratings may reduce visibility or lead to removal.
  • Featured placement is a paid service. Top placement is earned automatically from ratings (see section 8) and cannot be bought.

Posts and how long they stay up

  • Food posts (Stores): a food post is open for orders for the first 8 hours. Ememart reminds the Partner during the last 4 hours before it closes. After 8 hours the post shows as Closed and Customers can no longer order from it. The post, with its photos, is removed from Ememart 24 hours after it was posted.
  • “Leave open” food posts: a verified Partner may choose to leave a food post open. It then has no timer and stays up until the Partner removes it. Ememart sends the Partner freshness reminders (after 6 hours, at 8 hours, then every 8 hours). The Partner must only keep food up if it is safe to eat, and complaints about unsafe food can lead to suspension.
  • All other posts (Artisan services, items and pre-order bookings) stay up for 30 days, then are removed automatically. Ememart notifies the Partner 3 days and 1 day before.
  • When a post is removed, its photos are deleted from our storage. Orders already placed keep a record of the item name and price, so receipts and disputes still work, as explained in our Privacy Policy.
  • These times are the current settings. The app shows the time left on each post, and we will tell you in the app if they change.

5. Orders, payments and escrow

  • When you place an order, your payment is taken from your Ememart wallet and held in escrow. The Partner is not paid until the order is confirmed.
  • Escrow is released to the Partner when you confirm delivery in the app, or when the Partner or Rider enters the 6-digit delivery code that you give them at handover (payment is released immediately once the correct code is entered), or automatically after the period shown in the app if no dispute is open (currently 24 hours after delivery).
  • A Partner must accept or decline a new order within the time shown in the app (currently 30 minutes). If the Partner declines or does not respond in time, the order is cancelled and your payment is refunded to your wallet in full.
  • You can cancel an order at any time until the Partner accepts it, and your payment is refunded to your wallet in full. Once the Partner has accepted, only the Partner can cancel it. If there is a problem after that, message the Partner or open a dispute.
  • If an order is cancelled before fulfilment, or a dispute is decided in your favour, the held amount is refunded to your wallet in full or in part as decided.
  • Where a Rider delivers, the delivery fee goes to the Rider less Ememart’s service share. If no Rider is assigned, the Partner keeps the delivery fee.
  • Payments are processed by Squad. Wallet funding can use card, bank transfer or USSD. Third-party charges may apply.
  • Amounts are in Nigerian naira (₦).

8. Ratings and reviews

  • Only a Customer with a completed order can rate the Partner for that order. One rating per order. Star rating is required and a comment is optional.

  • “Top” Partners are ranked by average rating, with a minimum of 5 ratings, then by number of completed orders.
  • We may remove reviews that are fake, abusive, unlawful or unrelated to the order. We will not change a star average by hand.
